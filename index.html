About

I am currently a research fellow at the University of Birmingham, working with Sergey Goncharov. I enjoy seeing category theory being applied to various areas of computer science, including automata theory and semantics. My PhD was supervised by Corina Cîrstea at the University of Southampton, in which we explored the relationship between linear-time semantics and two-player games, using the theory of coalgebras.

Prior to embarking on a PhD, I worked as a computer scientist at Netcraft, an internet security company in Bath.
